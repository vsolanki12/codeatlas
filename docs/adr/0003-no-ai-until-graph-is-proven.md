# ADR-0003: No AI Until the Graph Is Proven

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 2026-07-14

## Decision

AI features (Ollama, RAG, embeddings) are not on any timeline. They happen when the Atlas Graph is complete and accurate.

## Why

AI without context hallucinates. AI with a complete, accurate graph becomes extremely precise. The graph is the hard part. The AI integration is comparatively straightforward once the graph exists.
